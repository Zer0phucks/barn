Newest map reveals who owns every property in California

This tool can help you investigate your landlord or any property in California

By Emma Stiefel | Updated Feb. 12, 2026 11:07 a.m.

Type in your address, or any other California address, to see who officially owns nearly any building. The map contains data most recently obtained from property records organization Regrid in October 2025. Most records were updated in 2025, but some were last updated in 2024. Click on a property to see the date it was last updated.

The map can also be used to see how ownership has changed in any year since 2023, when the Chronicle began collecting this information.

Search for an address or select a property on the map.

The San Francisco Chronicle has published this map so that residents can be more informed about who owns what in California. If you are an individual who owns fewer than three properties across the state and you do not wish for your name to be listed on our map, you can contact us at data@sfchronicle.com. The Chronicle will not remove the information of companies, organizations or landlords.

How the Chronicle made this map

In October of 2025, and in each of the prior years going back to 2023, the Chronicle obtained property data for all 58 California counties from Regrid, a company that aggregates and processes assessor rolls, or public information used to determine property taxes. The data includes owner names, mailing addresses, property addresses, and coordinates for most properties — details were missing for a small percentage.

The Chronicle processed the data to make it easier to match owner names and mailing addresses in spite of formatting variations — for example, cutting out punctuation and making all letters uppercase. This meant that our analysis could recognize “Apple llc” and “Apple L.L.C.” as the same organization.

We combined the processed data for each county into a single giant file. The Chronicle generated new columns that map each property to all other properties with the exact same owner name and mailing address — this data powers the “see other properties with the same owner” and “see other properties with the same mailing address” buttons in the map above.
